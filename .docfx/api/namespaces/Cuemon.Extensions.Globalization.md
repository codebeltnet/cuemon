Perform region-aware string comparisons, statistical region lookups, and locale-sensitive operations on `RegionInfo` and `StatisticalRegion` types through intuitive extension methods. Use these when you need to classify regions by type (country, natural region, or administrative region) or produce demographic and geographic markup.

Complements: [Cuemon.Extensions namespace](/api/extensions/dotnet/Cuemon.Extensions.html) 📘

### Extension Members

|Type|Ext|Methods|
|--:|:-:|---|
|RegionInfo|⬇️|`IsRegion`, `GetCultures`|
|StatisticalRegion|⬇️|`ToMarkup`, `GetDemographics`, `GetEconomy`, `GetGeography`|
|StatisticalRegionInfo|⬇️|`IsWorld`, `IsSubregion`, `IsIntermediateRegion`, `IsCountryOrTerritory`, `IsArea`, `HasIsoCodes`, `HasRegionInfo`|
